Nick Loves Judo!!!

My name is Nick, and I am a student at the Robert H. Smith School of Business at the University of Maryland. Outside of class, my favorite hobby is judo, a Japanese martial art built around throws, pins, and using your opponent's energy against them,

Judo keeps me in shape, but it has also taught me discipline and patience. The most important lesson I have learned on the mat is that falling is part of the process. Every time you get thrown, you get back up and try again.

My Favorite Judo Techniques:

  • Seoi Nage (shoulder throw)
  • Osoto Gari (major outer reap)
  • Uchi Mata (inner thigh throw)
  • Kesa Gatame (scarf hold pin)

How a Typical Judo Practice Works:

  1. Warm up and stretch
  2. Practice breakfalls (ukemi)
  3. Drill throws with a partner (uchikomi)
  4. Work on groundwork (newaza)
  5. Finish with live sparring (randori)
